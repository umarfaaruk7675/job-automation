    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    What does Shortlist do?

    It scans official career pages and job boards every day, scores each job against your profile, tailors your resume for the ones that fit, and applies for you automatically.

    Does the AI apply without asking me?

    Yes, for jobs where it has everything it needs. If any information is missing, the AI holds the application and waits for your answer before applying. It never guesses.

    What happens when information is missing?

    The application pauses and you get a short question, for example whether you have used a tool the job asks for. Answer once and the AI continues. Your answer is saved so it does not need to ask again.

    Does the AI invent experience on my resume?

    No. Tailoring only rewords and reorders what is already in your confirmed profile. It never adds a skill, employer, date or number that isn't yours.

    Which countries and job sources are covered?

    Shortlist is worldwide. Pick the countries you would work in, or choose remote only. Jobs come from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and several remote job boards.

    How do I sign in, and is my resume private?

    Sign in with Google, LinkedIn or GitHub. Your resume is private to your account and is used only to match, tailor and apply on your behalf. It is never used to train AI models.

    Shortlist is free while it is in beta and no card is needed to start.
